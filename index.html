Initiatives When Air Quality Is Poor

Practical steps for individuals, schools/colleges, and local communities in Dhaka:

Personal & Health

  • Use well-fitted KN95/N95 masks when PM2.5 is high.
  • Limit outdoor heavy exercise during peak traffic/morning smog.
  • Keep indoor windows closed during severe pollution hours.

Campus & Community

  • Run awareness drives on open waste & biomass burning.
  • Encourage native shade trees and indoor air-purifying plants.
  • Log daily local observations to build a clean dataset.

Structural & Policy

  • Enforce dust control (water spraying & covering) at construction sites.
  • Promote emission checks for old vehicles and brick kilns.
  • Support transparent, neighborhood-level air monitoring data.
